- Qwen3.8-27B-class engine on Metal: `serve --execution-witness` covers the 9B and the 27B (`qwen3_5-9b` and `qwen3_5-27b` on `metal/tp1/mlx-q4g64/text`). The witness binds every weight byte and the SHA-256 of every Metal library the load builds, with its compile options, the OS build and the chip. It stays off by default, and replies are unchanged with it on (#5184, refs #5177). Thanks to @kotevcode.
