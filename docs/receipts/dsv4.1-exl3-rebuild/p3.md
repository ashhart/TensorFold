## The arithmetic

- Routing is main's: `tf_dsv41_route_plan` gives, per expert slot, its (token, expert) pairs; pair q is token q / 6's
  q % 6-th pick. Experts are 16 here (`experts % 8 == 0`, 2 a combine group).
- Input rotation: main's `tf_exl3_rotate_in` in indexed mode, one row a pair (`repeat` 6 for gate/up, 1 for the down),
  with each pair's expert's suh: fp16((x * suh) through the seven butterfly stages * fp32(1/sqrt(128))).
- The slot dot: a slot's pairs share the expert's trellis, a simdgroup owns 32 columns, a lane one column, and runs
  passes of 4, 2 or 1 pairs; within a pass, each (matrix, pair) chain is `tf_exl3_matmul_row`'s: k ascending,
  product = fp32(x'_k) * fp32(W_q[k][c]) (exact), TwoSum next = fl(high + product), part = fl(next - high),
  low = fl(low + fl(fl(high - fl(next - part)) + fl(product - part))), high = next, sum = fl(high + low). Which pairs
  share a pass changes nothing in a chain, so each pair's sums equal `tf_exl3_matmul_row` run on it alone.
- The act: per pair, gate and up as `tf_exl3_rotate_out` writes them: the 128-wide butterflies, * fp32(1/sqrt(128)),
  * svh (sign or fp16), + bias, rounded to bf16; then main's MXFP4 epilogue: g = clip(gate, -inf, limit),
  u = clip(up, -limit, limit) with NaN kept, act = bf16(silu(g) * u * weight), silu(g) = g / (1 + precise::exp(-g)).
- The down: `tf_exl3_rotate_in` of each pair's act row with the down's suh, the slot dot with one matrix, then main's
  `tf_exl3_rotate_out` with `output_ids` = the plan's `where`, which writes bf16 `out` in main's per-pair layout; main's
  `tf_dsv41_xp_combine` (f32) or its bf16 form adds a token's six rows with weights of one.
- The serial reference emulates the GPU's fp32 flush-to-zero (Metal's fp32 add and multiply flush subnormal operands
  and results to a signed zero; fp16 and bf16 conversions keep subnormals) in the rotations and the epilogue, and
  calls P1's serial dot unchanged. Metal's `precise::exp` and divide are not reproducible on the host: silu differs
  from float64 rounded to fp32 at 3205 of the 65280 finite bf16 gates on M4 Pro. The reference therefore reads silu
  from a table that `tf_dsv41_x3_silu` fills once with the shipped function over all 65536 bf16 gates; every other bit
  is computed on the host.
- Mixed widths: an expert's gate, up and down may each have their own width and codebook; the bank gives mul1 at
  2, 3, 4 and 5 bits by expert (the down offset by one), or mul1 2.5, mcg 2 and 3inst 3 bits in turn.

## Gates and results

- Kernel contract, four registrations: up (gate, up and act) and down (with main's combine, f32 and bf16), mul1 at 2
  to 5 bits by expert or mul1, mcg and 3inst by expert, at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 16 rows (up k 1024 to n 256, down
  k 256 to n 512) and at one and four rows of V4.1's sizes (up 5120 to 2304, down 2304 to 5120); slot sums, act,
  expert outputs and combined rows equal the serial reference byte for byte (NaN as NaN) at every gate, and gate 4's
  mirrored stress rows sum to exactly 0.
- Lane: main's plan, up, down and combine at one row of V4.1's sizes and 2, 3, 4, 8 and 16 rows of 1024 and 256 (16
  rows also with mixed codebooks); every sum, act, output and combined row is bit-equal to the serial chain, and every
  pair's gate, up and down sums and outputs equal `tf_exl3_matmul_row` launched on that pair alone (P1's kernel) and
  routed `tf_exl3_matmul_hoisted` (main's) on all pairs.
- Controls, red at shapes: the down's TwoSum error term dropped (a slot sum differs at 1 x 512 x 256), the up's (a slot
  sum differs at 1 x 256 x 1024), and the act without the route weight (an act differs at 1 x 256 x 1024).
- float64, reported only: row 0 at eight columns against float64 from the fp16 rotated row on (no other rounding):
  max |gpu - f64| 6.9 at max |y| 3313 on M4 Pro; the lane rounds gate, up, act and each expert output to bf16, as
  main's MXFP4 lane does, and the float64 chain does not.
- M4 Pro, `tf-kernel-contract --only "x3 exl3"`: `SUMMARY 4 kernels: 4 pass gates 1-4, 0 fail, 0 known findings;
  2 controls: 2 red as planted, 0 missed; 0 limit problems; gates 169080 ms`; `--no-gates --only "dsv41 x3 "
  --offline DIR`: M1 and M2 build the three kernels for 32 threads.
- M5 Ultra: not run yet.
