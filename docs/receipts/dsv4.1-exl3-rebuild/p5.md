## Where main's select time goes (measured before building)

Kernel time a dispatch on M5 Ultra, `time-dsv41-metal-synth` (six synthetic layers at release widths, weighted to 40)
with its fixed position moved to 32,768 and 131,072 (a local edit of the harness, not committed), one row:

| context | `tf_topk512` a ratio-2 / ratio-1 index layer | 8 index layers | 40-layer round | share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 32,768 | 36.5 / 62 us | 0.42 ms | 19.8 ms | 2.1 % |
| 131,072 | 115.8 / 224 us | 1.47 ms | 22.2 ms | 6.6 % |

`tf_topk512` is one threadgroup a row: its time grows with the row (13 us at 1k keys, 220 us at 128k, 1.7 ms at 1M
on M5 Ultra) while the rest of the GPU waits behind the barrier that follows it. The FP4 scores of the same row take
566 us at 128k on M5 Ultra and spread over every core.

## The method

- A chunk is 32 consecutive keys of a row, the decode scorer's chunk (`encodeDecode`, `encodeDecodeSegs`): a
  simdgroup's lanes. `tf_topk512_bounds` runs four simdgroups a threadgroup, one chunk each, and writes each chunk's
  largest key under `tf_topk512_key` (NaN as -inf, -0 as +0); a padding key is 0, below every real key.
- With C > 512 chunks (rows past 16,384 keys), let T be the 512th largest bound. 512 chunks have a key at or above
  T, so at least 512 keys are, and the 512th largest key is at least T. Every key of the top-512 is therefore at or
  above T, and its chunk's bound is too. The candidates are exactly the keys at or above T.
- `tf_topk512_bounded`, one 1,024-thread group a row: T by `tf_topk512`'s three bin passes over the C bounds; then
  the candidates' indices in index order, 1,024 chunks a step (a simdgroup walks its 32 chunks' ballot bits and its
  lanes read the chunk's keys; a count, a barrier, a prefix, then the writes); then `tf_topk512`'s passes and ordered
  output over the candidates. Their order is the row's index order and they hold every key at or above the 512th, so
  the top-512 of the candidates, ties to the lower index, is the row's. Past 2,048 candidates (many ties at T), and
  for rows of 16,384 keys or fewer, the same group ranks the whole row as `tf_topk512` does.
- A simdgroup whose live keys share one bin adds them with one atomic (the -inf bin of masked keys, the bounds' top
  bins); the counts, and so the bins, are the same.
- The host takes this path when the launch's longest row passes 16,384 keys (`indexer.zig`): one slot's rows packed at
  their key count, or every slot's at the scratch's width; the bounds live in a new scratch of 4 bytes a chunk a row.

## Gates and results

- Contract, M4 Pro, `tf-kernel-contract --only "dsv41 topk"`: the pair registered as a replacement of `dsv41 topk512
  tf_topk512` passes gates 1-4, 27 shapes bit-equal to it (1, 4, 8 and 16 rows; 512 to 1,048,576 keys; visible counts
  0, 1, 512, 513, half and all; ties, ±0, NaN and ±inf; candidate-masked rows) and the stated rule against the CPU
  stable rank; three controls red as planted (NaN ranked above +inf at gate 1; a chunk's last key out of its bound
  and the keys equal to T out of the candidates at gate 3). `--only dsv41`: 121 kernels pass, 220 controls red, 0
  limit problems.
- Layer, `select_test.zig`: past 20,000 positions, each index layer's `sel` equals `tf_topk512` on the layer's own
  scores, one slot of 3 rows and two slots of 2 (ratio 1 bounded, the candidate-masked layer through the whole-row
  path, ratio 2 on `tf_topk512`).
- Kernel time, `tf_topk512` against the bound and select pair, one row of scores from main's FP4 scorer or random
  ReLU-like values (a local Swift harness over the two sources, results compared byte for byte), M5 Ultra: 32k keys
  58.9 / 30.1 us, 64k 113 / 33, 128k 222 / 39, 256k 436 / 49, 1M 1,724 / 106; four rows at 128k 223 / 43. M4 Pro:
  32k 94 / 39, 128k 231 / 37, 1M 1,797 / 116. At 16,384 keys and below the host keeps `tf_topk512`.
- Candidates a row on main's FP4 scorer's output at 128k keys (random FP4 keys and queries): 535 to 565 of 2,048.
- `time-dsv41-metal-synth` on M5 Ultra, one row, position moved as above, main against this branch: the eight index
  layers' top-512 1.47 to 0.35 ms at 131,072 and 0.42 to 0.30 ms at 32,768; the 40-layer round 22.17 to 21.10 ms
  (-4.8 %) and 19.79 to 19.62 ms (-0.9 %). Four slots of one row: 33.31 to 32.95 ms and 27.96 to 27.78 ms; that
  harness gives slots 1-3 all-zero scores (every key ties), which take the whole-row path.
